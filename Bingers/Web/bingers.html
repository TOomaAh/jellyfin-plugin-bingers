Import from bingers.app to Jellyfin

Control how watch history from bingers.app is brought into Jellyfin by the "Import watched history from bingers.app" task.

Check this to prevent Jellyfin from marking an item as played, even if it is marked watched on bingers.app. Items are never marked unplayed by the import.

Sync from Jellyfin to bingers.app

Control how your Jellyfin viewing activity is sent to your bingers.app account.

Instant Sync (As it happens)

When a movie or an episode is played to the end in Jellyfin, mark it as watched on bingers.app.
When you manually mark something as played in Jellyfin, immediately update it on bingers.app.
When you manually mark something as unplayed in Jellyfin (e.g. marked played by mistake), mark it as not watched on bingers.app.
Watching an already watched movie to the end increments its play count on bingers.app.
Watching an already watched episode to the end increments its play count on bingers.app.

Scheduled Sync (During background task)

The "Export watched history to bingers.app" task marks everything watched in Jellyfin as watched on bingers.app.

Advanced

For troubleshooting only. Writes detailed logs at the Information level (no server log level change needed): every bingers.app request, how each item is matched in the catalog, and what the scheduled tasks do with each item.
